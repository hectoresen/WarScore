## Why

Reduce decorative clutter in the lower rows of Classic Forever General while keeping the statistics visible.

## What Changes

- Remove the icons for duel wins, battlegrounds played, battleground wins, battleground win rate and duel win rate in Classic Forever General.
- Preserve both rows, all captions and values, and the favorite battleground icon. Center the five numeric values in their cells.
- Preserve the Classic Forever Simple layout and statistics.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `classic-forever-general-footer`: Defines the scope of lower-row icon visibility in Classic Forever.

## Impact

The Classic Forever stat band renderer changes. Confirm the five icon removals and the original text layout in WoW Forever.
