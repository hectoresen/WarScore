## Why

Help new players choose their WarScore appearance and language on first use.

## What Changes

- Show a single welcome screen on the first opening and reuse the four Settings controls.
- Save a completion marker when the player continues or skips. Preserve existing installations and show the welcome again after closing an unfinished session.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `first-run-setup`: Manage first opening and subsequent dismissal.
